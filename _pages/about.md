<tt>leane.jourdan@univ-nantes.fr</tt>


I am Léane Jourdan, a researcher in Natural Language Processing in France. I am part of the [TALN research group](https://taln-ls2n.github.io/) at the [LS2N laboratory](https://www.ls2n.fr/).

In december 2025 I defended my Ph.D.thesis at Nantes Université on Automatic text revision for scientific Writing Assistance. This thesis was supervised by [Richard Dufour](https://cv.hal.science/richard-dufour), [Florian Boudin](https://boudinfl.github.io/) and [Nicolas Hernandez](https://nicolashernandez.github.io/).

My research lies within the field of NLP and my main research interests are writing assistance, NLP4Science and NLP4education.
